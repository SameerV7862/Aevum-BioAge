# Literature Notes (Working Draft)

This file is a placeholder for curated evidence used by the bio-age model.

## Candidate evidence categories
- Muscular endurance and all-cause risk associations
- Functional lower-body performance and aging markers
- Movement variability / neuromotor control and age-related decline
- Isometric endurance and functional healthspan correlations

## Curation requirements
- Prefer systematic reviews, meta-analyses, large cohorts
- Record sample size, population, effect size, and limitations
- Tag by direct relevance to push-up and squat-derived features
